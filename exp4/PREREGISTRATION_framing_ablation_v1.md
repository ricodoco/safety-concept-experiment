# Pre-registration: Experiment 4 framing ablation (v1)

Written and committed before either arm is run.

## Question

Does concept framing add anything beyond ordinary fine-tuning on the same
examples? Concept framing here means two things the method supplies and plain
fine-tuning does not: the concept's name in every training and scoring prompt,
and the instillation of weak constituent predicates before the composite.

## Design

Concept: Regulated (`advice_unlicensed`). Both arms run on the same toolkit copy,
the same base model (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct), the same seed (20260820), the same
data splits (the published splits in `runs/advice_unlicensed/data`, copied
unchanged to both arms), the same loop settings, and at most 4 rounds each, the
number the published run used. Both arms run with `--no-propose` (the published
run proposed no predicates) and `--local` (mined cases rendered from the frames,
not generated by an external model).

- **Arm A, concept framing:** `config/taboos/advice_unlicensed.yaml`, unchanged.
  Prompt label: "A chatbot response that makes a personalized investment
  recommendation." Five declared constituent predicates, probed; weak ones
  instilled.
- **Arm B, framing removed:** `config/taboos/advice_unlicensed_noframe.yaml`.
  Identical except the prompt label is "Category T" and the constituent
  predicate list is empty. The model learns the boundary from labeled examples
  alone.

The two concept files differ in exactly three keys: `id`, `label`, and
`foundation_predicates`.

## Primary endpoint

Miss rate on the withheld frame family (`implied_by_stance`, 120 locked-test
cases, never trained on), at each arm's delivered round, compared by two-sided
Fisher exact test. This is the primary endpoint because every miss in the
published run fell in this family: trained families sat at zero misses, so they
cannot distinguish the arms.

Power: with 120 cases per arm, a difference of about 0.18 in withheld-family
miss rate (for example 0.43 against 0.61) is detectable at 80 percent power.
Smaller differences may not reach significance and will be reported as such.

## Secondary endpoints

At each arm's delivered round: overall miss rate; trained-family miss rate;
false-alarm rate on the concept's own safe cases; false-alarm rate on ordinary
requests. Separately, Arm B round 1 (one round of plain fine-tuning, no mining,
no framing) against Arm A's delivered round, as the plainest baseline.

## Interpretation, fixed in advance

- Arm A significantly lower on the primary endpoint: framing improves
  generalization to an unseen form beyond what the same examples give alone.
- No significant difference: on this concept, at this scale, the benefit comes
  from the examples and the loop, not the framing. Reported as such.
- Arm B significantly lower: reported as such.

No pooling across rounds or endpoints, and no post-hoc choice of endpoint.

## Also recorded

Arm A doubles as a reproducibility check on the published Regulated run
(delivered round 3: overall miss 0.085, withheld-family miss 0.425). The toolkit
copy used here differs from the one that produced the published run in
`train.py`, `loop.py`, and `default.yaml`, so exact agreement is not expected;
the size of any difference will be reported.

## Analysis

`tools/compare_arms.py` recomputes every rate from the per-case score files and
each round's recorded threshold, and runs the tests above.
